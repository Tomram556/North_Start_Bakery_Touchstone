Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery focused on bringing fresh, handcrafted baked goods to our local community. We believe a bakery should be a welcoming place where families, commuters, and neighbors can find something made with care.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe quality baked goods begin with thoughtfully selected ingredients. Our approach focuses on quality, freshness, and creating products that reflect the handmade character of our bakery.

Meet Our Team

Our bakery team works together to prepare fresh breads, pastries, and cakes while creating a warm and welcoming experience for every customer who visits North Star Bakery.

Our bakers focus on careful preparation and consistent quality, from everyday favorites to special-order baked goods.
